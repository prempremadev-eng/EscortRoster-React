Say each line aloud three times.

## Small words to notice

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| "**on** my friend's phone" | "in my friend's phone" |
| "**an** email" | "a email" |
| "**unique**" (yoo-NEEK) | "unic" |
| "**schedule**" (SHED-yool / SKED-jool) | "scheldule" |
