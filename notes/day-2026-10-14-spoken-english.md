1. "Today I designed a master list for all the security guards in every branch."
2. "Each guard belongs to one branch, and each branch belongs to a city and a state."
3. "I kept the escort duty sheet separate from the access card system."
4. "When a guard swipes in, they get a notification on their phone."
5. "Escort guards open the duty sheet from the notification and fill in their own trips."
6. "They can see everyone's row, but they can only change their own."

## Short dialogue (practice with a friend)

> **Supervisor:** "Raja, did your card show IN today?"
> **Guard:** "Yes, at seven forty-two. I got a message saying seat nine."
> **Supervisor:** "Good. Please fill in your route and out time on the sheet."
> **Guard:** "Okay. Can I change someone else's row?"
> **Supervisor:** "No, only your own row. The others are view only."
