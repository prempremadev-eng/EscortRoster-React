# Spoken English practice: 2 Nov 2026

Say each line aloud three times.

1. What happens if we keep two posts there?
2. Show the G shift only on days when someone has it in the roster.
3. If the duty guard can't add the reliever, the admin can do it.
4. We don't know who the reliever will be; we only find out that day.
5. Allow IN from forty minutes before the shift, and OUT only after it ends.
6. If they swap shifts, the admin should get a message and give permission.
7. Once the admin says OK, open his duty page without another scan.
8. The profile photo has nothing to do with the face photo; don't mix them.
9. Tap the photo to see it full size, and change it from the three-dot menu.
10. Is everything deployed to Production?
