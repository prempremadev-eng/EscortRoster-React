Say each line aloud three times.

2. The other agencies of the branch can look at the escort sheet, but not change it.

4. When I touch the mic once, it should keep recording without my finger.

9. Keep the date column fixed and let the shifts scroll sideways.
10. Let's discuss the profile photos later.
