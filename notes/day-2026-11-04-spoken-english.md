# Spoken English practice: 4 Nov 2026

Say each line aloud three times.

1. The guard has gone out, but the app still says he is on duty.
2. Can the owl turn its whole head, not only its eyes?
3. Please give the owl a little more time; the next page comes before it blinks.
4. Make this page look better on the phone and on the laptop.
5. The vehicle number should change to capital letters by itself.
6. Sometimes a post guard has to go on an escort trip at short notice.
7. He fills in the In time himself after every trip.
8. The parking report is due at eleven, three and eleven at night.
9. If an accident happens, the ERT guard should write a report.
10. Put a hide button there, so I can open it only when I need it.

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| he is still on duty | still on duty ena solgirathu |
| at short notice | urgent ah poganum |
| the report is due at eleven | 11 kku report podanum |
| if an accident happens | accident nadanthaal |
| only when I need it | thevai pattal |
