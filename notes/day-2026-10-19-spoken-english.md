# Spoken English practice: 19 Oct 2026

Say each line aloud three times.

## About the work

1. Today we finished the auto fill for all the posts.
2. The rules come from four months of old schedule photos.
3. The lady guards change their post every week.
4. On weekends, the permanent reception posts get the ladies first.
5. If nobody is free from the list, the app picks anyone who is free on that shift.
6. The app chooses the right people, but the supervisor still decides the exact post.

## Asking and explaining

7. Could you check who covered this post on Saturdays?
8. Let's take everyone from the history, most days first.
9. Please remove this post; we don't need it.
10. Taking a screenshot is hard because only half the page shows.
11. I'll show this rule book to my sir tomorrow.
12. I've been working on this for two days, so let's finish it today.

## Small words to notice

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| "**on** Saturdays" | "in Saturdays" |
| "**fewer** guards" (you can count them) | "less guards" |
| "**covered** the post" | "covered to the post" |
| "**history**" (HIS-tuh-ree) | "histry" |
| "I've **been working** on this for two days" | "I am working on this from two days" |
