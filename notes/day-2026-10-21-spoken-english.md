# Spoken English practice: 21 Oct 2026

Say each line aloud three times.

## About the work

1. Today we connected the escort duty to the monthly roster.
2. The escorts who are off on Saturday and Sunday go on the evening list.
3. Everyone moves four seats down every day, and it starts again on Monday.
4. Only the guards on the night shift do escort duty.
5. The guard taps his card and his phone shows his seat number.
6. We tested the whole chain with the card simulator, and it worked.

## Asking and explaining

7. Could you check which shift he was on in the real sheet?
8. Leave that slot empty; the supervisor will fill it.
9. Please hide this button, we don't need it any more.
10. Make the names a little bigger on the phone.
11. Is there any sensitive data on GitHub?
12. Will the notification work on an iPhone?

## Small words to notice

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| "**which** shift **was** he on?" | "he is in which shift?" |
| "it starts **again** on Monday" | "it again start Monday" |
| "**fewer** buttons" | "less buttons" |
| "a **little** bigger" | "little bit more big" |
| "**sensitive**" (SEN-si-tiv) | "sensitivity data" |
